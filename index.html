Should you get a cat?

Yes, get a cat.

Life will be better with a cat.

Cats are great companions and can be very loving.

Why?

Unlike dogs, cats are independent and low-maintenance(sometimes..?).

They're better for small spaces and aren't as noisy

They are very cute and lovable.

What type of cat?

It all depends on your lifestyle and preferences. But if you can it would be awesome to just adopt one!

These are some questions to ask yourself:

  • Do you want a long-haired or short-haired cat?
  • Do you want a kitten or an adult cat?
  • Do you want a specific breed or are you open to any cat?
  • How much time can you dedicate to your cat?
  • Do you want a cat that is more independent or one that is more social?
  • How big is your home?

All pets are equal and deserve love and care. They all just require different things. When getting a pet, it's important to consider your lifestyle and what you can provide for them. They're NOT just objects or decorations. They're also human beings and deserve to be treated with respect.
